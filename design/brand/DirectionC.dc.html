Review
Practise
Instrument, not verdict. Sample size and trend sit beside the number, so the figure is framed as a measurement.
Why this
The product's real claim is that it measures what you know. This brand makes that claim credible: tabular numerals, sample sizes, trends. It ages best as the data features land, and it suits the college student most.
The trade-off
Coolest of the three, and the least comforting at 11pm before an exam — a Grade 11 student may read it as another gradebook. Indigo-on-white is also the most common look in this category.
