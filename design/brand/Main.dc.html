Review
Practise
Warm paper, no red, no alarm. 42% reads as something to do, not a grade you were given.
Why this
Every AI study app looks like a bright purple SaaS dashboard. This looks like a good notebook — which is what students already trust. The warmth does real work: it lets the app tell you something unflattering without it stinging.
The trade-off
Quiet by design, so it will not feel exciting in a screenshot, and it gives up the momentum that streaks and celebrations trade on. A serif display also needs care at 13 px and below.
