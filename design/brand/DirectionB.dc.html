Review
Practise
Amber on pine: the bar reads as fuel rather than a warning. Big numerals make progress feel like a score.
Why this
Momentum is the thing students actually lack, and this brand is built to supply it — the footprint trail, the big numerals, the room for streaks and celebrations later. It is the most memorable of the three and the easiest to grow into gamification.
The trade-off
Energy cuts both ways: a bold brand makes 42% louder too, and it can tip toward a kids' app. The heavy display face also needs discipline to stay out of the UI.
